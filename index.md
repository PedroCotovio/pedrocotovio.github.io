## About

I am a PhD candidate in Informatics at <a target="_blank" href="https://www.lasige.pt/">LASIGE</a>, <a target="_blank" href="https://ciencias.ulisboa.pt/">Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon</a> (FCUL), currently a visiting PhD student at the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/schools/science-technology/computer-science">Department of Computer Science</a>, City St George's, University of London.

My research is on neuro-symbolic approaches to ontology matching and knowledge graph alignment: combining learned representations with symbolic structure, and making the resulting alignments interpretable enough for domain experts to validate. I work on both the systems that produce alignments and the benchmarks used to evaluate them.

I am supervised by <a target="_blank" href="http://www.di.fc.ul.pt/~catiapesquita/">Cátia Pesquita</a> (Associate Professor, FCUL) and <a target="_blank" href="https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/people/academics/ernesto-jimenez-ruiz">Ernesto Jiménez-Ruiz</a> (Senior Lecturer in Artificial Intelligence, City St George's).

Since 2024 I have led the Bio-ML track of the <a target="_blank" href="https://oaei.ontologymatching.org/">Ontology Alignment Evaluation Initiative</a> (OAEI), and I organise its new DISO track for 2026.

During my MSc I worked on deep reinforcement learning for n-way data analysis.

## Software

**EXACT-OM** — main creator. Interpretable, context-aware ontology matching combining lexical similarity, relation-specific subgraph context and language-model signals, with visual and natural-language explanations of each decision.

**Matcha** — contributor since 2022; OAEI participant. I maintain the Python reimplementation, `pymatcha`.

**LogMap** — contributor since 2025. I maintain the Python reimplementation, `pylogmap`.
